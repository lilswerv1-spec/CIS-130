My Top 3 Favorite Foods

Everyone has foods that they enjoy more than others. These are three of my personal favorite foods and some of the reasons why I enjoy eating them.

Fried Chicken

Fried chicken is a popular dish made by seasoning chicken, coating it in breading or flour, and cooking it until the outside becomes crispy and golden brown.

Why I like it: I like fried chicken because I enjoy its crispy texture and flavorful seasoning. I also like being able to enjoy it with different sides and sauces.

Alfredo Pasta

Alfredo pasta is a creamy pasta dish usually made with noodles and a rich sauce containing ingredients such as butter, cheese, and cream.

Why I like it: I enjoy Alfredo pasta because of its creamy texture and rich flavor. It is also a comforting meal that can be prepared with different ingredients.

Tteokbokki (Korean Rice Cakes)

Tteokbokki is a Korean dish made with chewy rice cakes cooked in a flavorful sauce. The sauce is commonly made with ingredients such as gochujang, which gives the dish its distinctive red color and spicy flavor.

Why I like it: I like tteokbokki because of its chewy texture and combination of spicy, sweet, and savory flavors.
